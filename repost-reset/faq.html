Help & answers.

Practical guidance for your next cleanup.

Getting started

Is Repost Reset made by TikTok?

No. It is an independent tool by GL Technologies, with no affiliation, endorsement or sponsorship from TikTok.

How do I connect my account?

Use Connect TikTok and sign in on TikTok’s official website. Complete any verification in that website. The app checks the connected account and syncs your lists. Never send your password, verification codes or cookies to support.

Why does sign-in need attention?

Website sessions may expire, and TikTok may require a challenge or verification. Open sign-in, complete the official steps, and return to the app. If the page stalls, use its refresh or retry control. Support cannot complete verification for you.

Why are counts missing or out of date?

Wait for sign-in verification, then use Sync. Cached lists are a previous snapshot; they do not authorize a new cleanup until the account and list have been checked. An incomplete or failed scan is not treated as an empty list.

Choosing & removing

What does cleanup remove?

It removes selected repost, like or favorite actions from the chosen list. The three categories are independent. It does not delete the creator’s original video, your TikTok account or unrelated entries.

Can I preview or select only some videos?

Yes. Tap a cover to preview. Use the selection controls for individual, monthly or all-item selection, and search by creator or caption. Search can hide selected items; review the selection-count menu before tapping Remove. Watching a video does not select it.

Which date is shown?

Grouping and sorting use the video’s publication date when available, not the time you reposted, liked or favorited it. Videos without a known date are shown separately.

Is there another confirmation after Remove?

In the current cleanup screen, tapping Remove starts the selected actions immediately. Review the category and the full selection first. Restoration is limited and cannot be guaranteed.

What do Removed, Pending and Retry mean?

Removed means the app obtained verification of removal. Pending means the result is uncertain and needs a check. Retry means the item was not confirmed removed or an error occurred. An accepted request, timeout or interrupted connection alone is not proof of removal.

Why is cleanup slow or temporarily limited?

Requests are deliberately spaced out. TikTok may apply additional limits and a cooldown; the app saves progress and waits. Respect the displayed wait, avoid repeated retries, and resume when allowed. A paid plan does not bypass TikTok limits or guarantee account safety.

Can cleanup continue with the screen locked?

For reliability, keep the app open in the foreground. iOS background time and website execution are limited; background or locked-screen completion is not guaranteed. After an interruption, reopen the app, check the task status, and continue if offered. Uncertain actions are checked before another attempt.

Why did an account change stop my task?

Tasks belong to the TikTok account that started them. Sign in to that same account to continue, or stop the task before starting another. A task is not transferred to a different account.

History, restoration & data

What can I restore from Recycle Bin?

Eligible verified removals can be restored by making a new repost, like or favorite action on the same account. Only a confirmed reappearance is marked Restored. Private, deleted or unavailable videos may not be restorable. Old timestamps, order and favorite folders are not restored.

What does Delete permanently mean?

In Recycle Bin it removes the local restoration entry. The original cleanup record can still keep its result and video metadata. Deleting a cleanup record removes that record and its related restoration entries. These actions do not delete a TikTok video or reverse a completed cleanup. Local archival backups can retain earlier records; see the privacy policy.

Does Disconnect delete my history?

No. It clears this app’s TikTok website session on this device. Local history remains, and completed TikTok actions are not undone. Disconnecting does not delete your TikTok account or cancel an Apple subscription.

Where is my data stored?

Account identifiers, video metadata, list snapshots, tasks, results and restoration records are saved on this device. Covers are cached locally. There is no app-managed cloud sync or automatic upload of history to a GL Technologies server. Device backups and third-party websites have separate behavior; see the privacy policy.

Why is a cover or video unavailable?

Media URLs can expire, connectivity can fail, or TikTok may remove or restrict a video. Sync again to refresh metadata, or open the original in TikTok. A placeholder cover is not proof that a video was removed from your list.

Purchases & support

Do I need Pro to clean up?

Cleanup currently works without a subscription and has no app-imposed free-action quota or selection-count cap. Pro does not grant access to another person’s account or bypass platform restrictions. Only purchase if the in-app offer suits your needs.

How do I restore or cancel a purchase?

Use Settings → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for purchase. For cancellation, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or use Manage subscription in the app when available. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple cancellation instructions.

Why are prices or purchases unavailable?

Store availability, connectivity and the installed distribution can affect Apple products. Retry later. Demo prices are examples and cannot charge you. The current public download is not linked on this website; actual product availability and charges are determined by Apple’s purchase sheet.

How can I get help?

Visit the support center or email GL.Tech@icloud.com. Include the app version, iOS version, selected category and a description of the issue. Redact private data first.

开始使用

Repost Reset 是 TikTok 官方应用吗？

不是。它是 GL Technologies 开发的独立工具，与 TikTok 没有关联、授权背书或赞助关系。

如何连接账号？

点击连接 TikTok，在 TikTok 官方网页中登录并完成验证。应用会确认当前账号并同步列表。请勿向支持人员发送密码、验证码或 Cookie。

为什么登录需要重新验证？

网页会话可能失效，TikTok 也可能要求验证。请打开登录页，完成官方步骤后返回应用；页面卡住时可刷新或重试。支持人员无法代替你完成验证。

为什么数量未显示或不是最新？

等待登录确认后使用同步。缓存列表只是上次快照，新的清理需要先确认账号和列表。扫描失败或不完整不会被当成空列表。

选择与移除

清理会移除什么？

它会取消所选类别中选中视频的转发、点赞或收藏操作，三个类别相互独立。它不会删除作者的原视频、你的 TikTok 账号或其他无关条目。

可以先预览或只选择部分视频吗？

可以。点击封面预览，通过选择控件选中单条、整月或全部内容，也可按作者或文案搜索。搜索可能隐藏已选条目，请在点击移除前检查选择数量菜单。观看视频不会自动选中它。

显示的是哪种日期？

分组和排序使用视频发布时间，而不是你转发、点赞或收藏的时间。日期未知的内容会单独显示。

点击移除后还会再次确认吗？

当前清理页点击移除后会立即执行。请先确认类别和完整的选择范围。恢复存在限制，不能保证成功。

已移除、待核验和待重试是什么意思？

已移除表示应用取得了移除核验证据。待核验表示结果不确定，需要检查。待重试表示未确认移除或发生错误。请求被接受、超时或连接中断本身都不能证明已移除。

为什么清理慢或出现限流？

应用会主动拉开请求间隔。TikTok 可能进一步限流并要求冷却；应用会保存进度并等待。请遵守显示的等待时间，避免反复重试，允许继续后再操作。付费不能绕过 TikTok 限流，也不能保证账号不受限制。

锁屏后还能持续清理吗？

为保持可靠性，请让应用保持前台。iOS 后台时间和网页执行能力有限，不能保证后台或锁屏后完成。中断后重新打开应用，查看任务状态，并在提供继续入口时恢复；不确定操作会先核验再尝试。

为什么账号变化会停止任务？

任务属于发起时的 TikTok 账号。请登录同一账号继续，或先停止旧任务再开始新的任务。任务不会转移到另一个账号。

历史、恢复与数据

回收站可以恢复什么？

符合条件的已核验移除条目可以在同一账号下通过重新转发、点赞或收藏恢复。确认重新出现后才会标记为已恢复。私密、已删除或不可用的视频可能无法恢复；原时间、顺序和收藏夹归属不会恢复。

永久删除是什么意思？

在回收站中，它移除本地恢复入口；原清理历史仍可能保留结果和视频元数据。删除整条清理记录会移除该记录及关联恢复入口。这些操作不会删除 TikTok 视频，也不会撤销已完成的清理。本地存档备份可能保留早期记录，详见隐私政策。

断开连接会删除历史吗？

不会。它清除本设备上应用的 TikTok 网页会话，本地历史保留，已完成的 TikTok 操作不会撤销。断开连接不会删除 TikTok 账号，也不会取消 Apple 订阅。

我的数据保存在哪里？

账号标识、视频元数据、列表快照、任务、结果和恢复记录保存在本设备，封面会本地缓存。应用没有自建云同步，也不会自动把历史上传到 GL Technologies 服务器。设备备份和第三方网站另有处理方式，详见隐私政策。

为什么封面或视频打不开？

媒体链接可能过期，网络可能失败，TikTok 也可能移除或限制视频。可以重新同步元数据，或在 TikTok 中打开原视频。占位封面不代表视频已从你的列表移除。

购买与支持

清理必须购买 Pro 吗？

当前清理无需订阅，也没有应用设置的免费操作次数或选择数量限制。Pro 不会授予他人账号访问权，也不能绕过平台限制。请根据应用内展示的权益决定是否购买。

如何恢复购买或取消订阅？

使用购买时的 Apple 账号，在应用设置中点击恢复购买。取消订阅可前往 iPhone 设置 → 你的姓名 → 订阅，或使用应用提供的管理订阅入口。删除应用不会取消订阅。Apple 取消订阅说明。

为什么价格或购买不可用？

Apple 商品可能受到商店地区、网络和安装发行版本影响，可稍后重试。演示价格只是示例，不会扣款。本站目前尚未提供公开下载链接，实际商品和扣款以 Apple 购买确认页为准。
