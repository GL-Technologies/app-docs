Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: October 13, 2026.

1. Who we are

Repost Reset is an independent iOS app provided by GL Technologies. Contact: GL.Tech@icloud.com. This policy covers our app and its support workflow. TikTok, sign-in providers, Apple and GitHub process data under their own policies.

2. TikTok sign-in and account operations

Sign-in occurs on TikTok’s official website in an embedded WebKit browser, and may open the sign-in provider you choose. You enter credentials into those websites; Repost Reset does not ask you to send credentials to GL Technologies. WebKit stores website session data on your device. The app uses the signed-in session to identify your account, read lists and perform the repost, like or favorite actions you select. Session credentials are not sent to a developer-operated server. TikTok and your sign-in provider receive the requests and may process technical information such as IP addresses and website cookies.

3. Data stored on your device

The local archive contains account identifiers and usernames, video IDs, creators, captions, original and cover URLs, available dates, list categories and snapshots, selected task items, cleanup and restoration results, request identifiers, timestamps and cooldown state. Covers are downloaded and cached locally; appearance, language and other preferences are also local. Archive files use iOS file protection. There is no app-managed cloud sync or automatic upload of these records to GL Technologies. iOS device backup and restore may retain app data according to your Apple settings; local storage does not mean exclusion from every device backup.

4. Covers, video playback and third parties

Covers and video previews connect to TikTok or its media hosts, which receive normal network requests. Playback uses a separate temporary WebKit session; management sign-in remains separate. TikTok website and player behavior, including their cookies or analytics, is governed by TikTok. The app does not integrate a developer analytics, advertising or AI service. Apple StoreKit processes purchases and provides product and entitlement information; we do not receive your card or bank details.

5. Notifications and Live Activities

If enabled and allowed by iOS, local notifications and Live Activities show task status and can open the associated cleanup. These displays can be visible on your lock screen. You can manage them in the app’s settings and iOS Settings. They do not guarantee continuous execution in the background.

6. Exports and support messages

If you export history or share a report, the chosen destination receives the content you send. History exports may contain account and video metadata. Contact us prepares an email draft with the topic, app version/build, device model, iOS version, connected-account state and Pro state. It is sent only if you send it using your mail app. GL Technologies and the email service process the message, sender address and attachments to handle your request. Avoid sending passwords, verification codes, cookies or unnecessary personal data. Contact us about information you previously sent and we will handle the request as applicable; we cannot delete data held independently by TikTok or Apple.

7. Retention and deletion

Disconnect clears this app’s website session on the device; it preserves local history and does not undo completed TikTok actions. Deleting a cleanup record removes it and related restoration entries from the active archive. Delete permanently in Recycle Bin removes the restoration entry while the original cleanup result and metadata may remain in history. Local archive recovery backups can retain earlier records, so these controls are not a secure erasure of every copy. To remove local app storage and its in-app recovery backups, delete the app rather than offloading it; separately manage device backups and any files, reports or emails you exported. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete your TikTok account.

8. This documentation website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and technical access logs under its privacy statement. This site uses browser local storage only to remember your language choice and includes no third-party fonts or analytics scripts. External links open services with their own policies.

9. Age requirements, rights and changes

Use the app only if you meet TikTok’s age and eligibility requirements and the requirements applicable in your region. For access, correction or deletion questions about information provided to us, contact our support email. Your applicable privacy and consumer rights are preserved. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date. See TikTok’s privacy policy or the regional policy shown in its website.

隐私政策

生效及最后更新日期：2026 年 10 月 13 日。

1. 我们是谁

Repost Reset 是 GL Technologies 提供的独立 iOS 应用。联系邮箱：GL.Tech@icloud.com。本政策说明应用及支持流程的数据处理；TikTok、登录服务商、Apple 和 GitHub 根据各自政策处理数据。

2. TikTok 登录与账号操作

登录在内嵌 WebKit 浏览器中的 TikTok 官方网页进行，并可能打开你选择的登录服务商网页。你在这些网站输入登录信息，应用不要求你把凭证发送给 GL Technologies。WebKit 在设备保存网站会话数据；应用使用登录会话识别账号、读取列表，并执行你选择的转发、点赞或收藏操作。会话凭证不会发送到开发者运营的服务器。TikTok 和登录服务商会接收请求，可能处理 IP 地址、网页 Cookie 等技术信息。

3. 设备上的本地数据

本地存档包含账号标识和用户名、视频 ID、作者、文案、原视频及封面链接、可用日期、列表类别及快照、任务选择项、清理及恢复结果、请求标识、时间和冷却状态。封面会下载并本地缓存，外观、语言等偏好也保存在设备。存档文件使用 iOS 文件保护。应用没有自建云同步，也不会自动把这些记录上传到 GL Technologies。iOS 设备备份和恢复可能根据你的 Apple 设置保留应用数据；本地保存不表示排除所有设备备份。

4. 封面、播放与第三方服务

封面及视频预览会连接 TikTok 或其媒体服务器，对方会收到正常网络请求。播放使用独立的临时 WebKit 会话，与管理登录会话分开。TikTok 网站和播放器的 Cookie、分析等行为受其自身政策约束。应用没有接入开发者分析、广告或 AI 服务。Apple StoreKit 处理购买并提供商品及权益信息，我们不接收银行卡或银行账户资料。

5. 通知与实时活动

你开启功能且 iOS 允许时，本地通知和实时活动可显示任务状态并打开相应清理任务，相关信息可能在锁屏上可见。你可在应用设置和 iOS 设置中管理。这些显示不保证任务在后台持续执行。

6. 导出与支持邮件

导出历史或分享报告时，你选择的接收方会获得你发送的内容。历史导出可能包含账号和视频元数据。联系我们会准备邮件草稿，包含问题主题、应用版本及构建号、设备型号、iOS 版本、账号连接状态及 Pro 状态；只有你通过邮件应用发送后才会送出。GL Technologies 和邮件服务商会为处理问题接收邮件、发件地址及附件。请勿发送密码、验证码、Cookie 或不必要的个人信息。对于此前发送的信息，你可联系我们，我们会按适用要求处理；无法替你删除 TikTok 或 Apple 独立持有的数据。

7. 保留与删除

断开连接清除本设备的应用网页会话，保留本地历史，不撤销已完成的 TikTok 操作。删除整条清理记录会从当前存档移除该记录及关联恢复入口。回收站永久删除只移除恢复入口，原清理结果和元数据仍可能保留在历史。本地恢复备份可能保留早期记录，因此这些控件不等于彻底擦除所有副本。要移除本地应用存储及其应用内恢复备份，请删除应用而不是卸载保留数据；另行管理设备备份，以及导出过的文件、报告和邮件。删除应用不会取消 Apple 订阅，也不会删除 TikTok 账号。

8. 本文档网站

本站托管于 GitHub Pages，GitHub 可能根据其隐私声明处理访客 IP 地址和技术访问日志。本站仅使用浏览器本地存储记住语言选择，没有第三方字体或分析脚本。外部链接对应服务有各自政策。

9. 年龄要求、权利与更新

只有符合 TikTok 及所在地区年龄和使用资格要求时才可使用应用。对于向我们提供的信息，访问、更正或删除相关问题可联系支持邮箱。适用的隐私和消费者权利不受影响。政策变更会在本页发布并更新日期。TikTok 的数据处理请查看其隐私政策或网站显示的地区版本。
