A clear cleanup flow

  1. Connect your own account through TikTok’s official sign-in website.
  2. Sync Reposts, Likes and Favorites. Choose a list and review the videos.
  3. Select the items you want to remove, then tap Remove. Removal starts immediately.
  4. Keep the app open. Check the result and recheck uncertain items.

History & Recycle Bin

History saves cleanup results locally. Verified removals can appear in Recycle Bin for a new repost, like or favorite action. Restoration depends on the original video and TikTok availability; it cannot recreate the old action date, order or favorite-folder placement.

清晰的清理流程

  1. 通过 TikTok 官方登录网页连接你自己的账号。
  2. 同步转发、点赞和收藏，选择一个类别并查看视频。
  3. 选中想移除的内容，点击 Remove（移除）后会立即开始。
  4. 保持应用在前台，查看结果并重新核验不确定的条目。

历史与回收站

历史在本地保存清理结果。已核验移除的内容可进入回收站，供再次转发、点赞或收藏。恢复取决于原视频及 TikTok 服务是否可用，不能还原原来的操作时间、顺序或收藏夹归属。
