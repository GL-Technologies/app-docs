💎 Subscriptions & Purchases

How do I restore my Pro/Premium purchase?
If you have previously purchased a Pro version or subscription, you can restore it by:
  1. Opening the Settings or Store page within the app.
  2. Tapping the "Restore Purchases" button.
  3. Ensuring you are logged in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel:
  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone/iPad.
  2. Tap your Name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Find the app and select Cancel Subscription.
Can I share my purchase with Family Sharing?
Most of our non-consumable Pro features support Apple Family Sharing. Ensure that Family Sharing is enabled in your iCloud settings and that "Share Purchases" is turned on.

☁️ Data & Syncing

How is my data synced across devices?
We use iCloud to securely sync your data across all your Apple devices.
Tip: Ensure you are logged into the same Apple ID and that iCloud Drive is enabled in your device settings.
Is my data backed up?
If iCloud Sync is enabled, your data is automatically backed up to your personal iCloud storage. We do not store your personal data on our own servers, ensuring maximum privacy.

🛡️ Privacy & Security

Do you collect my personal information?
Your privacy is our priority. Most of our apps operate on a "Privacy by Design" principle. We do not collect your personal content. For details on usage analytics, please refer to the Privacy Policy found in the app settings.
Can I lock the app with Face ID / Touch ID?
Yes, most of our apps offer an App Lock feature in the Settings menu to protect your sensitive information using your device's biometric authentication.

🔧 Troubleshooting

The app is crashing or not responding.
Please try the following steps:
  • Check the App Store for any available updates.
  • Force-close the app and relaunch it.
  • Restart your device.
  • If the problem persists, please contact us with your device model and OS version.
Feature X is not working as expected.
Ensure you have granted the necessary permissions (e.g., Camera, Photos, or Notifications) in the Settings app under the specific app section.
