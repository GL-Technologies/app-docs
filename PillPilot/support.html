Pill Reminder Support & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions and Help Center

💊 Medication Management & Safety

Does Pill Reminder provide medical advice?
No. Pill Reminder is an organizational tool designed to help you remember and track medication schedules. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment instructions, or drug interaction checks. Always follow the advice of your licensed doctor or pharmacist.
How does AI Medication Label Scanning work?
You can capture a photo of a medication box, pharmacy dispensing label, or prescription bottle. Pill Reminder's vision engine extracts the drug name, strength, dosage form, and directions.
Important: Always review and verify extracted details before tapping Save.
How does Voice Intake work?
You can tap the microphone button to dictate instructions (e.g. "Take 500mg Amoxicillin every 8 hours after meals"). Pill Reminder transcribes your voice on-device and organizes it into a reminder plan.
Can I manage inventory and low-stock warnings?
Yes! When adding or editing a medication, you can set your current stock count and unit. As you log doses, Pill Reminder automatically updates your inventory and alerts you when it is time to refill.

⏰ Reminders, Alarms & Live Activities

How do Strong Reminders and Alarms work?
Pill Reminder supports both gentle standard notifications and Strong Alarms (via iOS AlarmKit / Critical Reminders). Strong Reminders ensure critical doses are not missed even when your phone is in silent or Focus mode (when permitted).
Why did I miss a medication reminder?
Please verify the following iOS settings:
  1. Open iOS Settings > Notifications > Pill Reminder and ensure Allow Notifications and Time Sensitive Notifications are enabled.
  2. If you use Focus Modes (e.g. Sleep or Do Not Disturb), add Pill Reminder to the Allowed Apps list.
  3. Ensure your device is not completely powered off or out of battery.
How do Dynamic Island & Lock Screen Live Activities work?
When a scheduled dose window approaches, Pill Reminder can display an active countdown timer on your iPhone's Dynamic Island and Lock Screen, letting you log intake with a single tap.

🔒 Privacy & App Lock

Is my medical data stored privately?
Yes, absolutely. Your medication records are stored locally on your device in your private database. We do not operate advertising trackers or sell your personal data.
How do I lock Pill Reminder with Face ID or Passcode?
Open Pill Reminder, navigate to Settings > App Lock, and toggle on Enable Face ID / Passcode. Once enabled, biometric verification will be required whenever the app is opened.

☁️ iCloud Sync & Backup

How do I sync my medication data across devices?
Ensure you are logged into the same Apple ID on both devices and that iCloud Drive is enabled in iOS Settings. Pill Reminder uses Apple's CloudKit to seamlessly sync your records across your iPhone and iPad.
How do I restore my data if I switch to a new phone?
When you log into your new iPhone with your existing Apple ID and install Pill Reminder, your iCloud synced medication plans and logs will download automatically.

💎 Subscriptions & Purchases

How do I restore my Pill Reminder Pro purchase?
If you reinstalled the app or switched devices:
  1. Open Pill Reminder and go to Settings > Pill Reminder Pro.
  2. Tap Restore Purchases.
  3. Ensure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.
How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed directly by Apple:
  1. Open the iOS Settings app.
  2. Tap your Apple Account Name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Pill Reminder and tap Cancel Subscription.
Does Pill Reminder Pro support Family Sharing?
Yes! Non-consumable and eligible Pro subscription tiers support Apple Family Sharing. Make sure Family Sharing is enabled under your Apple Account settings.
