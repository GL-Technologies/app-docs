Privacy Policy

PillPilot Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026 | Last Updated: August 20, 2026

Welcome to PillPilot ("we", "our", or "us"), developed and operated by GL Technologies. PillPilot is an iOS application designed to help individuals and caregivers organize medication schedules, record intake, track inventory, and receive timely reminder alerts.

We understand that your medication schedules and health records are deeply personal and sensitive. This Privacy Policy explains how PillPilot collects, uses, handles, and protects your information in strict adherence to Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, App Store Review Guidelines, and modern privacy standards.

🛡️ Our Privacy Pledge: PillPilot is built on a "Privacy by Design" foundation. The app does not require account creation, does not display third-party advertisements, does not sell your personal data, and stores your health records locally on your device by default.

1. Information We Process & Permissions We Use

We strictly follow the principle of Data Minimization. We only request system permissions and process information that is directly necessary to provide medication scheduling and reminder features:

  • Medication & Schedule Records: All information you create (including medication names, dosages, units, taking frequencies, reminder times, inventory counts, expiration dates, and personal notes) is stored securely on your local device.
  • Camera & Photo Library (NSCameraUsageDescription / NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): If you use medication label scanning, barcode lookup, or prescription OCR intake, PillPilot requests access to your camera or photo library. Images are analyzed locally and ephemerally to assist you in filling in medication information. You always have full review and editing control before any information is saved.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription / NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription): If you use voice input to quickly dictate medication schedules, PillPilot uses Apple's Speech Recognition framework to convert your voice to text. Audio is processed for real-time transcription and is not permanently recorded or saved on external servers.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (NSFaceIDUsageDescription): If you enable the App Lock feature to secure your medication lists, authentication is performed on-device via Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Biometric data is strictly confined to your device's Secure Enclave; PillPilot never has access to your raw biometric data.
  • Notifications & Alarms (NSAlarmKitUsageDescription): To deliver timely reminders and critical alarms, PillPilot schedules local notifications and alarms on your device.
  • Live Activities & Widgets: PillPilot uses Apple's ActivityKit and WidgetKit to display next-dose countdowns and status on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island based on local schedules.

2. AI Recognition & Privacy Minimization

When you utilize AI-assisted intake (e.g. intelligent prescription OCR or voice parsing):

  • Privacy Filtering: PillPilot automatically applies privacy minimization before sending text to our secure processing gateway, stripping or redacting identifiable patient names, personal addresses, phone numbers, and clinician account details.
  • No Secondary Use: Data sent to the AI processing gateway is used solely in real-time to parse medication attributes (e.g., drug name, dosage, frequency) and is immediately discarded. It is never used for commercial profiling or advertising, nor used to train public generative AI models.
  • Secure Transmission: All communications between the app and the AI Gateway are encrypted end-to-end using TLS (HTTPS).

3. Data Storage & iCloud Synchronization

  • Local-First Storage: Your primary database is stored directly on your iOS device via Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • Apple iCloud Sync: If enabled, PillPilot utilizes Apple CloudKit to synchronize your records across your personal Apple devices. Your data is encrypted and tied exclusively to your personal Apple ID account. GL Technologies does not operate separate proprietary servers holding your personal health data.

4. Drug Reference Data Lookups

PillPilot provides informational reference data (such as drug information from public catalog services like openFDA or cached reference catalogs). When you search for reference information, anonymous queries containing only the drug name or national drug code are sent. No user identity, location, or personal schedule data is transmitted during reference queries.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

PillPilot Pro subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit 2. We do not process, store, or have access to your credit card information, bank details, or billing address. Apple manages all financial transactions in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Third-Party Tracking & Advertising

PillPilot does not contain third-party ad networks, tracking cookies, or commercial data brokers. We do not track your activity across third-party apps or websites.

7. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your medication data:

  • You can view, edit, or delete any medication entry, schedule, or dose log within the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app permanently removes all local data stored on your device.
  • If iCloud Sync was enabled, you can manage or delete your iCloud backup data via iOS Settings > [Your Apple Account] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

8. Children's Privacy

PillPilot is not intended for use by children under 13 without parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
